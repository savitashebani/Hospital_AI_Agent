Hospital Management System

Hospital Timings:
The hospital is open from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.

Appointment Rules:
Patients should book appointments in advance.
Each appointment lasts 30 minutes.
A doctor cannot have two booked appointments at the same time.

Cancellation Policy:
Patients can cancel a booked appointment before the appointment time.

Departments:
Cardiology - Heart-related treatment
Dermatology - Skin-related treatment
Orthopedics - Bone and joint-related treatment

Patient Guidelines:
Patients should arrive 15 minutes before their appointment.
Patients should carry their previous medical reports if available.
